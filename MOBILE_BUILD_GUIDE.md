# Build Viggu Audio from your phone

1. Open GitHub on your Android phone and visit this repository.
2. Open the **Actions** tab and choose **Build Viggu Audio APK**.
3. Tap **Run workflow** and confirm.
4. Wait for the run to finish successfully.
5. Open the run summary and download `viggu-audio-debug-apk` from Artifacts.
6. Extract that artifact ZIP and tap `app-debug.apk` to install.

This builds a debug APK, intended for personal testing. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager.

If the build fails, open the workflow run, copy the error from the failed step, and share it for troubleshooting.
